PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 01 January 2026

SkillForge Vocational Training Institute ("SkillForge", "we", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and how we use and safeguard it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including when you submit an enquiry, enroll in a course, or contact our team. This may include:

  • Your name, email address and telephone number
  • Your course of interest and preferred batch timing
  • Any message or additional details you choose to share
  • Account details for the SkillForge student portal

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to enquiries and process enrollments
  • Provide training, assessment and certification services
  • Match graduates with relevant employment opportunities
  • Send you updates about batches, dates and job leads you request
  • Improve the quality and safety of our programs

3. Sharing Your Information

We never sell your personal information. We may share limited details with trusted placement partners only where you have asked us to support your job search, or where required by law.

4. Data Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, alteration or disclosure. Access to personal data is restricted to staff who need it to do their jobs.

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time. You can also opt out of marketing communications while continuing to receive essential course information.

6. Cookies

Our website uses minimal browser storage to remember your theme and direction preferences. This information stays on your device and is not used to track you across other websites.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at privacy@skillforge.edu.
