Our Story

FROM ONE
WORKSHOP

SkillForge started in a single rented workshop with eight learners and one welding machine. The mission was simple: teach the skills employers actually needed, and help every graduate find work.

Today we train thousands of learners across six trades, partnered with hundreds of employers, and still measure our success by one number — how many graduates are employed.

What Drives Us

MISSION, VISION
& VALUES

Our Mission

To deliver practical, employer-aligned trade training that takes people from no experience to a paying job in months, not years.

Our Vision

A world where a skilled trade is a respected, well-paid, first-choice career — and where training always connects to real opportunity.

Our Values

Safety first, practice over theory, respect for every learner, honesty about outcomes, and a stubborn focus on getting people hired.

Our Journey

MILESTONES
ALONG THE WAY.

2011

SkillForge Founded

Opened a single workshop with eight learners and a focus on welding and fabrication.

2014

Placement Desk Launched

Hired our first placement officer and built the employer network that still drives our outcomes today.

2018

Six Trades, One Campus

Expanded into electrical, plumbing, HVAC, solar and automotive with dedicated training bays.

2026

1,200+ Graduates Employed

Reached a 90% placement rate and a network of 250+ hiring partners across multiple industries.
